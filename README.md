## What is it?

This is my 3D-modeled Marble Run track! It's made up of three main parts:
* A wall of obstacles
* A spiral
* Tracks lead to them all

## How was it made?

I used the beginner [Starbie project](https://halflife.hackclub.com/) as my guide, and all the modeling was on Onshape! I first designed my marble run in Canva, where I was deciding what I could do.

<img width="1202" height="667" alt="Screenshot 2026-10-17 211251" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1bd6c7fb-1027-41ae-a569-563cb965294f" />


## Why was it designed this way?

I looked at other peopels designs for some inspo, and I decided I really wanted to do a spiral and an obstacle wall. I loved how some people had two way your marble could go, so I integrated that in too!

Currently, CAD files are complete! I'm waiting until a couple of weeks when I can start assembling!
